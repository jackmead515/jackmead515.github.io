About

I am an engineer at heart with a passion for my work. Growing up in Iowa, surrounded by corn fields and gardening with my family, I developed a love for the environment. My entire career, I've been focusing on wrangling data together for agronomists and environmentalists, scientists, farmers, and business professionals whether on the land or in the sea. Although it's hard to estimate, I've helped in the collection of billions images for computer vision, petabytes of telemetry from sensors all over the world, and trained models with billions of parameters

Here are some highlights from my professional work:

Long-term distributed timelapse solution for phenotyping
Custom stereographic camera with real-time video processing capabilities
Mask-RCNN model trained on hand-gathered dataset highlighting errors of disease on coffee leaves
Data pipeline performing pose detection and stereographic length estimation
Optimized stereographic homography matching for fish tracking
Underwater calibration and stereographic depth estimation from an ROV mounted camera
Segmentation, tracking, long-term reassociation, species identification, and counting of fish
Custom CNN+LSTM classification model of high-frequency sounds from marine mammals using spectrograms
GAN based synthetic generation for improved object detection model accuracy

As a life long student, I'm constantly learning and expanding my skillset. The environment will always remain a passion of mine and whether or not I'm working in the field, I will always be working to protect it. If your organization is in need please reach out to me at my email.
